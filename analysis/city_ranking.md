# City ranking: where to start a Latin activation, and where to start a new congress

Generated by `python scripts/city_ranking.py` (do not edit by hand). 24 cities: the 15 project cities and every city with 8+ Latin events in the census.

## Method

Each indicator is turned into a percentile rank between 0 and 1 across the 24 cities (a missing value counts as the median and is flagged below). A block is the mean of its indicators; a view is the mean of its blocks (equal weights). Indicators marked (-) count against a city.

- **Red Bull entry:** Latin scene = Latin events in the city, 3 years (census), Latin events with 1,000+ dancers within 150 km, Dance schools and venues (OpenStreetMap); Red Bull on the ground = Red Bull dance events 2024-2026, Red Bull office in the metro, Hyrox races in the city, 3 years (census); Reach = Population, Share aged 20-39 (%), Air passengers a year
- **New congress:** Demand = Latin events in the city, 3 years (census), Dance schools and venues (OpenStreetMap), Population, Share aged 20-39 (%); Access = Air passengers a year, Tourism nights a year (region); Room in the market = Latin events with 1,000+ dancers within 150 km (-), Latin events in the country, next 12 months (census) (-); Cost = Price level (country, EU = 100) (-)

Robustness: 2000 random sets of block weights (each block can weigh from almost 0 to almost all) give each city a median rank and the share of draws in which it is in the top 3 and top 5; leave-one-out removes one indicator at a time and shows the best and worst rank the city reaches.
